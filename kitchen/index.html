Know what goes into the numbers

Daily price updates

Market prices are a published snapshot; automatic daily refresh is awaiting approval. Supplier prices remain the imported snapshot. Rice: India Prices. Vegetables: Vegetable Market Price (retail range midpoint). Dal: India Prices (only unambiguous varieties). Each row shows the source's date. An old listing remains old even after a successful refresh. Salem rice and unlisted rations require a supplier quote.

The date picker shows actual records on past dates and estimates up to seven days ahead. No record means no price. “Today / latest” shows the last available value. Daily history accumulates from source updates. Entered supplier quotes are stored on this browser. The imported supplier list is undated and shown separately from market prices; its prices do not establish what the supplier charged on a selected historical date.

Projection & colour guide

Seven-day estimates use a linear trend only with at least three observations over two or more days from the same source and price basis within 30 days; daily movement is capped at 3% of the latest price. Otherwise the last price is carried forward as a labelled flat estimate. Older baselines are flagged. These are planning estimates, not guaranteed prices or validated statistical forecasts.

Green marks the lower price in each supplier/market pair, red the higher price, and grey equal or uncomparable prices. Difference = supplier minus general market; percentage uses general market as the denominator. Units must match. Named rice varieties are never inferred from unspecified white rice. Supplier unit rates are calculated from the quoted pack quantity and total cost, including gram-to-kilogram conversion. Costs exclude missing prices, tax, delivery, fuel and wastage. Contingency is separate.
