Know what goes into the numbers

Market references

Rice: India Prices, dated 17 Oct 2026. Vegetables: Today Price Rates, retrieved 1 Nov 2026; page contains conflicting dates (29–31 Oct). Vegetable values use the listed retail range midpoint. Confirm with your vendor.

Planning assumptions

Each future week = current basket × (1 + weekly price change) raised to the week number. Contingency is added separately. Missing prices are excluded and flagged. Delivery, tax, fuel and wastage are excluded. Supplier prices override references locally.
