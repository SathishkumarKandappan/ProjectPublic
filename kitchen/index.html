About prices & dish suggestions

Refresh reloads the published price feed and selects the latest recorded market price on or before your chosen date. The actual source date is shown below every market price. Missing records stay blank. Future dates and projections are not used. The feed is a dated snapshot; automatic daily updates are not currently enabled.

Your supplier list is an approved snapshot with no quote date. It is shown as an undated reference, not a confirmed historical or live supplier price. Undated supplier quotes are not used to rank dishes for past dates. Kilograms, litres, pieces, packets and bunches stay separate. White rice is not assumed to be Salem or Sona Masuri.

Sources: Rice prices · Dal prices · Vegetable prices. Vegetables use the midpoint of the listed retail range. Supplier and retail grades may differ.

Dishes are vegetarian Indian menu ideas. State or region labels indicate where a dish is known, not exclusive origin. Featured ideas rotate within a pool of lower-priced main ingredients, plus discovery picks. Missing ingredient prices never count as zero. Costs for the rest of a recipe, cooking method, nutrition and school dietary requirements still need review.

Regional references: Karnataka Tourism · Kerala Tourism · Incredible India: Gujarat.
