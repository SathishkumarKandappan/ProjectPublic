Know what goes into the numbers

Daily price updates

Public market listings are checked automatically each morning. Rice: India Prices. Vegetables: Vegetable Market Price (retail range midpoint). Each row shows the source's date. An old listing remains old even after a successful refresh. Salem rice and unlisted rations require a supplier quote.

The date picker shows actual records on past dates and estimates up to seven days ahead. No record means no price. “Today / latest” shows the last available value. Daily history accumulates from source updates. Supplier quotes are stored on this browser and override market prices on the same date.

Projection & colour guide

Seven-day estimates use a linear trend only with at least three observations over two or more days from the same source and price basis within 30 days; daily movement is capped at 3% of the latest price. Otherwise the last price is carried forward as a labelled flat estimate. Older baselines are flagged. These are planning estimates, not guaranteed prices or validated statistical forecasts.

Green means below that ingredient's target, red means above it, and grey means equal or no target. Targets start at the original reference and can be edited. Costs exclude missing prices, tax, delivery, fuel and wastage. Contingency is separate.
